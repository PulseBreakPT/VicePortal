# Vehicle Progress evidence audit — 2026-11-28

The existing GTA Town public data download (https://gtatown.com/creators/data, CC BY 4.0) is the bulk source. We extract only the era/decade, controlled body-style descriptors, named specialist workshop and the exact base model when the latter is uniquely present in the existing catalogue. These are source-reported facts, not reconstructed screenshots or exact menu-style labels. No copied source prose or imagery is included.

## Independent checks

- https://allthings.how/gta-6-how-many-vehicles-cars/ reports 277 base entries and 281 Standout entries. It also distinguishes menu era and style labels from workshop specialties. Used for spot checks, not as a redistributable table.
- https://allthings.how/gta-6-rare-cars-standout-vehicles-locations/ reports fixed locations for Standout models. Limited named location facts were cross-referenced against GTA Town map entries; citations distinguish the reported in-game location from the mapped area.
- https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-vehicles-cars/ reports 277 base and 280 Standout entries; its aircraft subpage lists 16 base aircraft. The one-Standout difference is unresolved between the guides (Street Racer: 16 vs 17). No claim that the 560-record VicePortal feed exactly equals either menu total. No deletion based on this discrepancy.
- https://gtaspotter.com/en/guides/gta-6-vehicles/ reports tuning ratings and some prices with confidence. Selected reported registration costs, stolen resale prices and race rental fees are attributed as such, rather than relabelled as purchase prices. No unconfirmed GTABase-derived tuning rows are promoted to confirmed performance. Existing scores are retained.

## Rights and scope

AllThings.How and PowerPyx reserve rights in their content (https://allthings.how/terms-and-conditions/ and https://www.powerpyx.com/tos/). No permission for bulk republishing their data collections was found. GTA Spotter has no verified open-data licence for the complete table. We retain links and limited individually researched factual annotations, not bulk scrape or redistribute those sites' articles, screenshot assets, compiled tables, or original descriptions. Existing independently licensed GTA Town data remains attributed. Additional facts require individual evidence or a permitted export. The source counts do not constitute a complete verification of every missing field.
