# GTA Town creator data integration

Source: https://gtatown.com/creators/data (CC BY 4.0). Published counts at 2026-11-27: vehicles 560, weapons 202, cheats 61, trophies 55, missions 195, challenges 80, collectibles/pickups 4,021, locations 2,889; total 8,063. All eight public exports are requested once per build, JSON first then CSV; validated rows and raw fields are cached in creatorData.json and persisted after a successful main deployment. Invalid, reduced or inaccessible responses retain the prior snapshot. Existing vehicle IDs and reviewed images are preserved by the existing vehicle importer. Weapons with an exact title match retain their old ID and media. Other datasets produce searchable dossier records with all source-supplied fields, explicit attribution and neutral placeholders. Spoiler-flagged imported entries are hidden from discovery and direct routes until enabled. Coordinates are displayed as source data; no coordinate transformation or map placement is inferred. No third-party game imagery is downloaded under the data license. Initial snapshot is empty until a network-enabled build succeeds; advertised counts are not claimed as imported. CI logs provide actual per-dataset counts and rejection reasons.
