# Universal Data Engine

Pinned source snapshots, per-source licenses and provenance. GTADB and Open GTA 6 Map named places are adapted to dossiers, with original coordinate spaces explicitly separated. Shared landmark IDs are cross-referenced, exact names and areas are required to merge existing locations, and ambiguous or explicit unresolved aliases enter review. Unknown names are not invented. The MIT radio catalogue is adapted to station metadata (no audio or lyrics), and CC BY price rows are labelled historical. Existing records keep IDs, images, paths and populated fields; conflicts are recorded instead of overwritten. Source registry records all previously identified repositories, including restricted or duplicate upstream sources without copying their content. Full upstream radio notices are included. No third-party images were copied. The engine is deterministic and uses pinned checked-in source snapshots; refreshing snapshots requires revalidation and licence review. No claim of complete cross-source deduplication or full coverage. GTADB unnamed landmarks, Open GTA 6 Map location/pickup file, technical hashes, and unresolved sources are not published by this pass.


## Follow-up validation

The successful production build reported 7,503 raw creator rows across seven datasets, 424/559 reachable unique vehicle references and 135 missing images. The vehicle export was rejected for absent class fields. This patch separates identity from classification: missing titles and duplicate IDs still reject the feed; exact reviewed model/manufacturer matches retain their existing class, and otherwise unknown classes are explicit. An export that drops reviewed IDs is rejected before caching. The CI artifact now includes an integration audit against actual creator rows (not the small isolated radio fixture), recording source revisions, conflicts and review candidates. The audit excludes legacy editorial/vehicle records and does not claim that cross-source duplicates have all been resolved. Open GTA 6 Map's larger location file remains outside this pass; its exact blob could not be retrieved through the connector. No unlicensed media or technical dump is imported.
