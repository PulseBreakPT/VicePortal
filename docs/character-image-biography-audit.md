## Evidence policy

- Official cast credits establish the credited label and performer, not a biography or appearance.
- Images of related scenes and radio station artwork have their own caption and source, and do not count as character portraits.
- No actor photographs, unrelated GTA-game portraits or speculative AI images were added as GTA VI character likenesses.
- New context uses named gameplay transcripts and individual character pages. No claim is made that an entire life history is available.
- The seven data test bodies passed through an assertion harness (6,117 assertions). Live remote image rendering and the complete frontend build have not been verified. GitHub Actions execution must be checked separately.
